How it works

From upload to insight in 3 steps

No configuration. No boilerplate. Just answers.

01
Upload your dataset

Drop a CSV or JSON. FeynML accepts datasets up to 50MB and automatically detects column types, target variables, and data structure.

02
Run diagnostics

Choose from drift detection, label noise analysis, leakage detection, calibration audit, fairness check, or a full pipeline scan.

03
Get actionable reports

Receive a detailed failure report with root cause explanations, severity scores, and fix recommendations — exportable as PDF.

Features

Engineered for Accuracy

Comprehensive diagnostics across the entire ML lifecycle.

Drift Observability

Monitor covariate and target drift with automated KS-tests and PSI metrics. Get per-feature severity ratings and historical drift timelines at a glance.

Calibration Audit

Identify miscalibration in probability scores and improve model reliability before production deployment.

Leakage Detection

Prevent target leakage and ensure your model generalizes to real production data. Surface contaminated features automatically using mutual information and SHAP-based signals.

Fairness Audit

Measure demographic parity, equalized odds, and disparate impact across all sensitive groups in your dataset.
