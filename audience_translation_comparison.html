Identical fields

  • Evidence strings are identical in all 9 audience outputs.
  • Recommendations are identical in all 9 audience outputs.
  • Source investigation confidence remains 85 for all audiences.
  • Source investigation risk level remains HIGH for all audiences; it is rendered explicitly in 6 of 9 views.

Audience-specific fields

  • `executive_summary` wording differs for all 9 audiences.
  • `findings` titles, status labels, severity labels, and notes differ by audience.
  • `impact_assessment`, `confidence_explanation`, and `technical_notes` are audience-specific.

Remaining overlaps

  • `2 evidence-backed issues were identified.` appears in executive summaries for 9 of 9 audiences.
  • `Assessment confidence: 85%` appears in findings for 9 of 9 audiences.
  • `Risk level: HIGH` is rendered for ML Engineer, Executive, Loan Officer, HR Manager, Insurance Analyst, and Legal / Compliance, but not shown in Student, Doctor, or Researcher views.

Suggested improvements

  • Render `risk_level` explicitly for all audiences so the comparison is visible without inferring from the source investigation.
  • Vary the shared summary phrase so all executive summaries do not repeat the exact sentence `2 evidence-backed issues were identified.`
  • Differentiate confidence framing further by audience, especially between ML Engineer, Doctor, and Researcher.
  • Consider an optional `audience_glossary` field so Student and Executive outputs diverge more in terminology while preserving evidence.
